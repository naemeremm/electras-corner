our framework

Grid2Plug (G2P)

A systems-thinking approach to clean energy education.

Grid2Plug connects the full clean energy ecosystem — from the power grid to the EV. It introduces the people, components, and systems that make electric mobility possible.

G2P is designed to inspire curiosity, build confidence, and create clear pathways into the clean energy workforce.
