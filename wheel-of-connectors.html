Type 1 (J1772)

North America

The Type 1 connector, also known as J1772, is the standard for AC Level 1 and Level 2 charging in North America.

T1
🌐
Region North America

Primarily used in the U.S. and Canada.

⚡
Charging Type AC Level 1 & Level 2

Not intended for DC fast charging.

🔌
Power Range Up to 7.4 kW (Level 2)

120V Level 1 / 240V Level 2.

🚙
Common Vehicles Nissan LEAF, Chevy Bolt, older Ford, Toyota Prius Prime, and more
ⓘ
Key Facts
  • 5-pin connector: 2 power, 1 ground, 2 communication.
  • Used for home, workplace, and public AC charging.
  • Often paired with CCS1 for DC fast charging.
